## Shared direction

Use the three recent Drazna images as visual world references only: preserve
their grounded painterly realism, wet late-medieval material language, cold
lake atmosphere, peat-black / silver-blue / restrained crimson / amber
palette, and practical construction. Create a new character; do not copy Queen
Mara's identity, face, crown, or costume.

All concepts are full-body game NPC anchors. They use natural anatomy, avoid
text, logos, watermarks, magic, elaborate armor, and generic high-fantasy or
steampunk styling.

## Ilya Sorn

A junior floodwarden of Drazna whose compassionate mistake helped open a
forbidden maintenance channel beneath the drowned city. Show him in an
operational Crown Sluice gallery with wet stone, timber gearing, chains, a
brass water gauge, and cold lake light. He is a lean man around thirty with a
tired, earnest face, damp practical hair, charcoal and lake-blue wool, leather
reinforcement, compact tools, a junior river-key clasp, rolled plans, and one
glove stained with black silt. His mood is capable, conscientious, anxious, and
sympathetic rather than sinister. He is a grounded late-medieval engineer, not
steampunk.

## Nera Bell

Keeper of the House of Names, who secretly asked divers to recover the census
of citizens erased from Drazna's official flood memorial. Show her in the dry
archive on Birch Heights among vellum rolls and bone-white name tablets, with
birch branches and the drowned bell tower beyond a window. She is a composed
woman in her mid-forties with warm brown skin, silver-threaded dark hair under
a simple archival veil, bone linen and soot-gray wool, ink-stained fingers, a
narrow chain of office, mismatched memorial tablets, and old records. Her mood
is quiet moral courage and disciplined grief. Her clothing is archival and
secular, not clerical.

## Olek Var

A charming salvage captain and Low Lantern intermediary who financed an
illegal dive beneath the Mud Crown and possesses one page of King Odran's
missing ledger. Show him on Lantern Quays at blue evening beside a narrow
salvage skiff, wet piers, ropes, recovered slate, and amber lamps. He is a
broad-shouldered man in his late thirties with a weathered expressive face, a
repaired eyebrow scar, an easy calculating smile, a patched peat-black boat
coat, muted crimson vest, waterproof boots, rope gloves, compact salvage tools,
claim tags, and a sealed oilskin document case. He is charismatic,
opportunistic, useful, and morally flexible, but not a pirate.

## Pava Mirek

Master roofwright of the Walking Ward and older sister of a diver missing
beneath Old Drazna. Show her high on an inhabited bridge under repair, among
black-timber houses, slate roofs, ropes, braces, and unfinished supports. She is
a powerful woman in her late thirties with a wind-reddened practical face,
cropped curly dark hair, charcoal, bone-linen, and faded lake-blue workwear, a
reinforced apron, climbing belt, measuring rule, hammer, and wedges. A
restrained crimson strip around her wrist belongs to her missing brother. Her
mood is resilient, direct, protective, and exhausted but unbroken. She is a
craftswoman and community leader, not a warrior.
