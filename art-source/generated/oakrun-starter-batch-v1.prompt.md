each image was its subject block followed by the shared production block below.

## Shared production block

Input images: Image 1 is the Oakrun style, palette, material, and lighting reference only; do not reproduce its surrounding town scene.

Style/medium: polished hand-painted grounded dark-folklore game sprite, readable silhouette, non-isometric, no tile base

Lighting/mood: restrained late-afternoon amber light from upper left

Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background for local removal

Constraints: one isolated subject only; background uniformly `#ff00ff` with no gradient, texture, floor plane, scenery, cast shadow, contact shadow, reflection, or haze; generous padding; no ground patch, text, logo, watermark, glowing magic, runes, UI, or extra objects; do not use `#ff00ff` in the subject

Avoid: isometric tile, diamond base, generic high-fantasy ornament, steampunk, painterly background
